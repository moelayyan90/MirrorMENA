## Evidence discipline

OpportunityOS will not convert an email sent, a page visit, a demo run, a verbal expression of interest, or an unpaid deliverable into a revenue/customer claim. A `customer` is recorded only after an arm's-length organization or user accepts the product/service, and `realized revenue` is recorded only when payment is actually received or an enforceable approved payout is confirmed.
